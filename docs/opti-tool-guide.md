## 1. Topic Selection Framework

### A. Viral Topic Research Checklist

**Step 1: Identify Trending Keywords**

Source data collection: Google Trends, YouTube search auto-suggestions, Reddit threads, TikTok trending sounds (leads YouTube by 2-3 weeks), Twitter/X trending hashtags, Quora "People also ask", YouTube Shorts trending videos.

Scoring system (1-10):
- Search volume increase: /3
- Competition level (lower = better): /3
- Audience size potential: /2
- Monetization value (CTR potential): /2
- Threshold: 6+ = viable topic

**Step 2: Niche Saturation Analysis**

Check top 10 videos: total views, average views/video, month-over-month growth rate, new channels breaking in (last 90 days), audience comment sentiment.

Saturation rating:
- Views < 50M total = GREEN (opportunity)
- Views 50-200M = YELLOW (competitive)
- Views > 200M = RED (saturated)

### B. Topic Score Card Template

Virality factors (checkboxes): emotional trigger potential, problem-solution format, story-based angle, actionable learning value, controversial/debatable element, personal transformation element.

Audience demand: search volume up in last 30 days, 5+ possible variations, evergreen + timely balance, monetizable audience (high RPM niche).

Competitive edge: faceless format optimal, unique unsaturated angle, format differentiation possible, B-roll/stock footage available.

Viability score /15:
- 12-15: GOLD (launch immediately)
- 9-11: SILVER (develop angle)
- 6-8: BRONZE (monitor)
- <6: PASS (skip)

## 2. Viral Video Analysis System

### A. Competitor Video Breakdown Template

For each top-10 viral video: title, channel, subscribers, views, likes, CTR estimate, upload date, performance velocity.

- **Hook analysis (0-5s):** opening line, emotion triggered, hook type, effectiveness, why it works.
- **Retention pattern:** 0-10s hook intensity, 10-30s story/problem setup, 30-60s key info/solution hints, 60-end conclusion/CTA, plus estimated drop-off points.
- **Script structure pattern:** hook / problem statement / story-evidence / solution-insight / closing-CTA, each with duration, total length, pacing speed.
- **Language patterns:** 5 trending phrases used, tone.
- **B-roll strategy:** cut types, animation speed, cut frequency, text overlay density.
- **Engagement tactics:** 3-5 specific tactics noticed.

### B. Pattern Recognition Across 10 Videos

Extract winning patterns present in 7+/10 videos (hook type, optimal length range, pacing, CTA position %, emotion focus, story structure), phrases appearing in 5+ videos, and unique differentiators from the top 1-2 videos.

## 3. Script Structure Templates

- **Template A — Problem-Solution** (most viral; self-improvement/education/psychology): Hook 0-3s, pattern interrupt 3-8s, problem deep dive 8-45s, solution explanation 45-120s, closing framework 120-end. Optimal length 10-13 min, medium-fast pacing.
- **Template B — Story-Lesson** (high retention; motivation/life lessons): Attention hook 0-3s, context setting 3-15s, turning point 15-60s, lesson unfolds 60-120s, application to viewer 120-end. Optimal length 12-15 min, slow-medium pacing, conversational tone.
- **Template C — Data-Insight** (algorithm favorite; finance/business/tech): Data hook 0-2s, why it matters 2-8s, context/background 8-25s, deep analysis 25-120s, action steps 120-end. Optimal length 11-14 min, fast pacing, heavy text overlays.
- **Template D — Top-X Listicle** (easiest to produce; tips/tools/hacks/reviews): Hook 0-3s, pattern interrupt 3-8s, intro setup 8-15s, list items 15-120s, closing 120-end. Optimal length 9-12 min, fast rhythmic cuts.

## 4. Hook Formulas (Proven & Trending)

**Tier 1 — highest viral potential (70% of time):** controversial question, shocking data/number, pain point mirror, curiosity gap (8-8.5/10 CTR boost).

**Tier 2 — strong performance (25% of time):** personal story opener, contrarian statement, bold promise (6.5-7/10 CTR boost).

**Tier 3 — moderate performance (5% of time):** simple direct statement (5/10 CTR boost).

**Hook formula combinations:** Question+Data, Story+Contrarian, Pain+Solution Teaser, Data+Personal, Curiosity+Urgency.

**Hook testing:** create 3 variations, score emotion/type/estimated CTR potential each, select the highest-scoring + strongest emotional hit.

## 5. Optimization Checkpoints

- **Pre-writing:** topic trending?, 10 top videos analyzed?, unique angle identified?, strongest hook formula selected?, optimal length planned?, story structure decided?, 5+ trending phrases identified?, B-roll strategy listed?, CTA position planned?, retention targets set?
- **During writing (every 100 words):** conversational language, short sentences (8-12 words avg), story/evidence/data every 30s, trending phrases used naturally, appropriate pacing, no filler, continuous value, shareable moments. Engagement spike required every 30-45s.
- **Post-writing:** readability (flows spoken, no awkward pauses, rhythm, tone, grade level 6-8), engagement (hook tested, duration right, first 15s attention, micro-retention per section, compelling CTA), value delivery (clear core message, strong evidence, actionable steps, reinforced conclusion, bonus surprise), viral potential (emotional journey, shareable moment, clear benefit, stands out, "would friends send this"). Score /13: 12-13 ready, 10-11 minor tweaks, 8-9 needs work, <8 rewrite.
- **Viral angle checklist:** emotional, educational, entertainment, relatable, shareability, monetization (RPM) angles — 5+ checked = ready for production.

## 6. Performance Metrics & Tracking

- **Pre-production estimation:** based on topic viability (/15), hook strength (/10), structure optimization (/10), viral angle coverage (/6) → predicted CTR range, 50% retention time, end screen retention %, 24h/7d view estimates, long-term potential, confidence level.
- **Post-upload tracking dashboard:** 24-hour metrics (views, likes, CTR vs estimate, retention at 50%/25%, comments, RPM estimate) and week-1 metrics (views, growth, avg view duration, retention, likes ratio, comments/sentiment, traffic source breakdown) plus performance analysis and takeaways.
- **Niche performance baseline:** videos analyzed, average CTR, average retention at 50%, average length, best/worst performing video, average RPM, your performance vs baseline, improvement strategy (low CTR → fix hook/thumbnail; low retention → fix pacing/edit speed; low RPM → target higher-value keywords).

## 7. Implementation Workflow (Weekly)

- **Monday:** research 3 trending topics (30m), analyze top 10 videos for #1 topic (60m), identify unique angle (30m).
- **Tuesday:** script outline (30m), full script writing (90m), script optimization (30m).
- **Wednesday-Thursday:** video production (editing, B-roll, effects).
- **Friday:** upload + optimize metadata (title/description/tags/thumbnail A/B), monitor first 24 hours.
- **Ongoing:** track metrics daily for 7 days, analyze results, document learnings, apply to next video.

## 8. Advanced Techniques

- **3-Video series strategy:** problem identification → solution detailed → advanced/bonus, cross-promoted CTAs, 30-60% view increase across the series.
- **Retention hack formula:** micro-hooks every 45-60s ("this next part is where it gets crazy...") placed right before likely exit moments; reduces drop-off 15-25% at each point.
- **Comment hack:** rewrite statements as engagement-forcing questions; increases comments 40-80%.
- **Thumbnail-script alignment:** script must make the thumbnail's promise come true — no false promises, higher retention + more subs.
- **Seasonal pivot:** every 30 days re-check trending topics, hook styles, optimal length, audience preferences, competitor strategies; update templates and phrase library.

## Quick Reference

**Best hook types by niche:** Education (data-insight, curiosity gap, contrarian), Motivation (personal story, pain point mirror, bold promise), Finance (shocking data, problem-solution, contrarian), Self-improvement (problem-solution, story-lesson, curiosity gap), Technology (data-insight, controversial question, contrarian).

**Optimal video lengths:** Problem-Solution 10-13m, Story-Lesson 12-16m, Data-Insight 11-14m, Top-X Listicle 9-12m, Tutorials 8-11m, Shorts 15-45s.

**Pacing guidelines:** Slow (15-20s cuts) for story/emotional/philosophical content; Medium (10-15s cuts) for educational/mixed content; Fast (5-10s cuts) for list-based/trending/listicle content.

**Common mistakes to avoid:** generic hook, slow first 15 seconds, no clear value proposition, filler words/silence, skipping competitor research, ignoring trending phrases, CTA at the end only, no story or evidence.

**Weekly optimization cycle:** Weeks 1-2 testing phase, weeks 3-4 optimization phase, weeks 5-6 scaling phase, weeks 7-8 refinement phase.
